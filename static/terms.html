1. What this service does

crowdin-stats generates two SVG images per registered Crowdin project — a translation progress table and a contributor grid — for embedding in READMEs or other Markdown documents. It reads data from the Crowdin API using a token you provide and re-renders it as an image on request.

2. No account, no warranty of continuity

There is no user account or login. Access to your badge configuration is controlled entirely by the unguessable public_id in your badge URLs and by your Crowdin token itself. Because there is no account recovery mechanism, losing your badge URLs means re-running setup.
